Tariff Welfare Realignment

1. Structural Framework

This framework explores the domestic market distortions created by trade protectionism. When a government imposes a tariff on imports, domestic prices artificially rise. While this shifts revenue to domestic producers and generates government tariff revenue, it reduces total consumer surplus and introduces unrecoverable Deadweight Loss (DWL), shrinking net economic welfare.

3. Documented Quantitative Framework

Below is a breakdown of the social welfare losses caused by a fixed 25% protectionist levy on imported industrial metals:

  • Consumer Surplus Deficit: -$12.4 Million (Higher retail prices)
  • Domestic Producer Premium: +$5.1 Million (Increased market share)
  • Treasury Revenue Collection: +$4.3 Million (Duties collected on remaining imports)
  • Net Societal Deadweight Loss: -$3.0 Million (Unrecoverable efficiency loss)
