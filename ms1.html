Understanding the Knapsack Optimization Model

Why model a pizza quantitatively? To turn subjective intuition into systematic resource allocation.

The Scarcity Problem: You want to build a premium pizza that maximizes total satisfaction. However, you face a strict constraint of exactly $7.00. Choosing toppings at random leads to budget overruns or low-value combinations. Using Binary Variables (1 = select, 0 = drop), you can create a mathematical model to find the absolute optimal choice under scarcity.
