Deconstructing the Autarky Debate Framework

1. Concept Evaluation: What is Autarky?

Autarky is a state of total macroeconomic self-sufficiency where a nation-state chooses to exist entirely independent of international trade networks[cite: 3]. In a pure autarkic system, all consumer utilities, industrial capitals, and commodity inputs must be derived exclusively from within sovereign geographic borders[cite: 3].

The Core Argument For Autarky

Proponents argue that by disconnecting from global supply vectors, an economy shields itself from external market shocks, geopolitical blockades, foreign labor exploitation, and currency volatility. It prioritizes absolute state control over shared network efficiency.
