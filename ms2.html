📖 Simplified System Overview

What are we solving?
Imagine you are running a global smartphone brand. You build your phones inside two massive manufacturing hubs in Asia (**Beijing & Shanghai**).

Customers across world capitals (**New York, London, Tokyo**) want to buy these phones immediately. Moving boxes by aircraft costs money, and every route has a completely unique tariff code pricing.

The Logistics Goal:
We want to satisfy every single store order while keeping our global air freight expenditures at the absolute lowest baseline point, all without overloading what each factory is capable of assembling per day.
