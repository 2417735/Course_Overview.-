🧩 Network Node Strategy

As demonstrated in the video clip above, optimization apps drop unnecessary floor details, transforming sorting aisles into unified Nodes and Weighted Paths to solve routing calculations fast.

🟠 S-Shape (Serpentine Wave) Strategy

Method: Loops seamlessly through entire aisles, entering from one crossway and leaving from the other to ensure continuous high speeds.

🟢 Largest Gap Heuristic

Method: Finds the biggest empty space inside an aisle. The picker treats that gap as a dead end, entering from the closest outer boundary wall to reduce useless interior travel.

🟣 Midpoint Splitting Heuristic

Method: Cuts the facility layout horizontally into upper and lower fields. The picker sweeps one half clean before moving across to finish the opposite side.
