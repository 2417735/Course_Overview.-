Heuristics & Spatial Mapping Logic

Why choose heuristic approximations over standard calculations?

🧩 Why Heuristics Matter

When solving the Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP) inside a facility, computing exact mathematical balances takes long data periods. Heuristics give picking staff human-executable instructions that eliminate on-the-floor hesitation.

🟠 S-Shape (Serpentine Wave) Strategy

Method: The driver loops seamlessly through entire aisles, entering from one crossway and leaving from the other. It treats aisles as a continuous fluid line to ensure high speeds.

🟢 Largest Gap Heuristic

Method: Evaluates the single biggest empty space between vertical target coordinates inside an aisle. The picker treats that gap as a dead end, entering from the closest outer boundary wall to reduce useless interior travel.

🟣 Midpoint Splitting Heuristic

Method: Cuts the facility layout horizontally into upper and lower operational fields. The picker sweeps one complete segment clean before traversing across to finish the opposite half.

🎬 Network Graph Mapping Insight: As modeled in the Network Analysis Lecture ↗, spatial tracking systems strip away warehouse decoration, changing coordinate points into active Nodes and Weighted Edges to run pathfinding logic safely.
