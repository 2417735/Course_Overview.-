Comparative Advantage Matrix

1. Structural Framework

David Ricardo's classical theory argues that international trade patterns are not determined by absolute efficiency, but by variations in relative opportunity cost. Even if a highly developed nation produces all commodities more efficiently than its trading partner, mutual macroeconomic gains are unlocked if each country specializes exclusively in the sector where their relative margin of superiority is highest.
