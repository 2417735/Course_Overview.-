The Gravity Model of International Trade

1. Concept Overview

Inspiration & Context: This analysis was developed based on core reading assignments evaluating global macroeconomic interactions, trade-to-GDP indices, and geometric spatial market boundaries across sovereign nations.

Core Concept Demonstrated: The Gravity Model predicts bilateral trade flows by assuming that trade volume is directly proportional to the product of two countries' economic sizes (GDP) and inversely proportional to the geographical distance separating them.

Industry Relevancy: In global supply chain optimization and logistics management, this model dictates foreign direct investment (FDI) paths, multi-national distribution node layouts, and helps isolate hidden institutional trade frictions caused by regulatory non-tariff barriers or physical infrastructure limits.
