Heckscher-Ohlin Factor Endowments

1. Structural Framework

The Heckscher-Ohlin model moves beyond labor hours to explain how geographic resource concentrations shape trade. It asserts that a country will export goods that intensively use factors of production it has in abundance (such as machinery, technology, or arable land) and import goods that intensively use resources that are scarce domestically.
