Each project focuses on a different advertising principle and explains how the concept can be applied in real-world marketing situations.

Final Reflection

Most Interesting Project

The Advertising Ethics Analyzer was the most interesting because it explored how advertising can influence society and consumer trust.

Most Challenging Project

The Media Planning project was challenging because it required balancing campaign goals, audience reach, and budget limitations.

Most Valuable Project

The AIDA Model project demonstrated how effective advertisements guide customers through a structured decision-making process.

How My Understanding Changed

Before this course, I viewed advertising mainly as creativity and promotion. Through the semester, I learned that successful advertising relies on research, consumer psychology, strategic planning, and ethical responsibility.
